FRIDAY
A short story
Shaurya Anjney
Friday — 00:00

Friday night · The loop begins

Friday I remember my name… no. I don't.

A call. A mansion swallowed by dark.
A door that simply ceased to exist.
And a smile — "Welcome home."

Scroll
01

I remember — yeah, I remember. My name is… No. I don't remember my name.

At least I remember how it started. My oldest memory? A faint shimmer — a smile, a hug too warm for this world. Even though I've only lived here for 24 hours, it feels like days. Or even years. I have no idea.

I remember it was a Friday night when all this started. Which week. Which date. No idea.

02

I was coming from work. It was late. The streets were screaming a quiet agony I didn't understand at that moment.

I could hear the faint hum of music in my EarPods. I was walking alone — no one was on the streets. Yet I felt an unsettled cold in my spine.

03

Then came the call. A colleague. A quiet woman — no one really talked to her except me. We were close friends, yet I always felt something was bothering her.

She was a woman of light brighter than the sun. A smile warmer than summer. Her presence — a comfort. A symbol of life itself.

When I picked up, she was sobbing. Not crying. Not weeping. Sobbing.

Her voice — afraid
Come to my home! I am not safe! Something… something is just not right! Please come to my home, I feel like I will——
And the line went dead.

I ran.

04

Her mansion — usually a symbol of beauty. That night the beauty felt stolen. Erased. It felt like the whole place had been abandoned long ago.

No guards. No lights. Completely different than usual.

I slipped past the gate. It gave a low, eerie creak. Its touch — wet and rusted.

05
Tak-tak tak.

The sound echoed from inside — as if the whole house were hollow. No one came. I tried her number. Three rings. No answer. A dread settled in my stomach.

My hand found a rock. The window broke before I realized what I was doing. Inside — dead-black. Silent as a prison of its own pain.

Then the clock chimed once — from inside.

It was 12 a.m. The start of Saturday.

06

I tried the door. It unlocked with a soft click. I went inside and said — "Hello? Anyone?"

Nothing.

I reached back for the door.

The door wasn't there.

07

It didn't close behind me. It simply disappeared. I was standing alone in darkness with no exit.

Then I heard her. Muffled. Distant. I followed the sound for around five minutes through the dark hallway. It kept getting clearer. Closer.

Then I saw her — a shadowy figure. Sitting near her phone. Hair tattered. Sobbing.

She was speaking on the phone. The words were crystal clear now.

The exact same words
Come to my home! I am not safe! Something… something is just not right! Please come to my home, I feel like I will——

She turned towards me. Smiled calmly.

"Welcome home."

Then — I don't know what happened.

I remember nothing.

Wait. What was I even saying?

Epilogue

I enjoy it here.
I trust her.

I don't know — these days I am forgetting a lot. Making up stories. I need to relax.

As of now I am just crashing in my colleague's house. It's been only 24 hours but I don't know — it feels like years.

My colleague is really sweet. She doesn't even let me go out. She says it's safe in here. She takes care of everything.

I think I'll just enjoy my Friday night.

— Shaurya Anjney
